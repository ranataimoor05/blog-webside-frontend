Life Lately

Embracing the shift in seasons.

Lately, everything has felt like a transition. The leaves are changing, the air is getting crisper, and my daily routines are shifting to match.

I've been spending more time indoors, brewing tea, and finally catching up on the pile of books that has been sitting on my nightstand for months. There is a specific kind of quiet that comes with this time of year, and I'm learning to lean into it rather than rush past it.
